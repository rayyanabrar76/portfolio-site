Rebuilding the website for Advanced Power Solutions

APS is a power engineering company in Model Town, Lahore, working since 2010. They supply generators, switchgear panels, UPS systems, solar systems and air compressors across Pakistan. I rebuilt their website from the ground up so customers can browse the full range and request a quote online.

01 / The brief

What the client needed.

APS hired me to rebuild their company website. They sell heavy-duty power equipment, and each product line has many models, sizes and brands. The website had to show all of that clearly, on a phone or a laptop.

The other goal was getting enquiries. Customers should be able to pick the products they need and send one quote request, and the APS team should be able to update products and answer requests themselves, without calling a developer.

02 / What I built

The features.
Built end to end.

Full product catalog

Generators sorted by engine brand (Cummins and Perkins) and power range, plus sections for switchgear panels, UPS systems, solar systems and air compressors.

Cart and quote requests

Customers add the products they need to a cart and send a single quote request, instead of calling about each item.

Customer accounts

Customers sign in with Google and can see their past quote requests in their profile.

Admin panel

The APS team manages products, quote requests, customers and blog posts from a private admin panel.

Blog

A built-in blog with an easy editor, so APS can publish news and guides about generators, solar and UPS.

Local Lahore page

A page made for customers searching in Lahore, alongside the company and services pages.

03 / Built with

The tools,
and why I used them.

Next.jsFast pages that load quickly and are easy for Google to read.
PostgreSQL + PrismaStores the products, customers and quote requests.
Google sign-in (Auth.js)Simple, secure login without another password to remember.
Tailwind CSSClean, consistent design that works well on phones.
Tiptap editorLets the APS team write and format blog posts in the admin panel.
