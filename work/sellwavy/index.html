Sellwavy, an online store builder

Sellwavy is my own product. Anyone can sign up, pick a theme and open an online store on its own address, like yourstore.sellwavy.com or their own domain, then sell with Stripe and run everything from one dashboard.

01 / The idea

Why I built it.

I built Sellwavy to show how a real multi-store platform works: one app that runs many separate stores, each with its own products, customers, payments and web address.

The same app serves three things: the Sellwavy website and owner dashboard on sellwavy.com, every store on its own subdomain, and stores that use their own custom domain.

02 / What I built

The features.
Built end to end.

A store on its own address

Each store gets a subdomain like yourstore.sellwavy.com, or can connect its own domain.

Themes and custom design

Store owners pick a theme. Paid plans can add their own CSS and HTML.

Payments to each store

Stripe Connect sends each sale straight to that store owner.

Subscription plans

Free, Basic and Pro plans billed through Stripe, with a 14-day free trial.

Full store dashboard

Products and variants, orders, customers, discounts and shipping, all in one place.

Shopper accounts and emails

Shoppers can sign in to each store, and order emails are sent automatically.
