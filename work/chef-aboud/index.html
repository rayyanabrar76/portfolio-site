A catering website for Chef Aboud Küche

Chef Aboud is a chef in Berlin who caters events. I built a premium website in English, German and Arabic where customers can choose a buffet package, or build their own, and order catering for their event.

01 / The brief

What the client needed.

Chef Aboud’s customers speak German, English and Arabic, so the website had to feel natural in all three, including Arabic, which reads right to left. The client also wanted it to look more premium than a typical catering website.

During the project the client decided to focus on catering only. I changed the website from restaurant ordering to a catering order flow, and kept the menu as a showcase of the food.

02 / What I built

The features.
Built end to end.

Three languages

English, German and Arabic, with the whole layout flipping right to left for Arabic.

Buffet builder

Customers pick one of three ready buffet packages, or build their own from food stations, and set the number of guests.

Catering checkout

Customers enter the event date, time, place and guest count when they order.

Menu showcase

The full menu and signature dishes, shown as a showcase so customers can see the food before they order.

Smooth motion

Gentle animations that give the site a polished, magazine-like feel.
