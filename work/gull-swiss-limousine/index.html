A booking website for Gull Swiss Limousine & Tours

Gull Swiss Limousine & Tours is a chauffeur company in Switzerland. I built their website so customers can book an airport transfer, ski transfer or tour and pay online, in English, German, French or Italian.

01 / The brief

What the client needed.

Their customers are travellers arriving in Switzerland, many of them on a phone. So the website had to work in four languages, show the services clearly and let people book and pay in a few simple steps.

The owner also needed one place to see every booking and customer, and to publish blog posts, without help from a developer.

02 / What I built

The features.
Built end to end.

Four languages

Every page in English, German, French and Italian, with the right language chosen from the address.

Step-by-step booking

Choose the ride, add passenger details, pay, and get a confirmation. Built to be quick on a phone.

Online payments

Secure card payments through Stripe, so bookings are paid before the ride.

Transfers, ski transfers and tours

Separate pages for each service, pages for popular transfer routes, and a page for WEF Davos transfers.

Fleet and FAQ

Pages that show the cars and answer common questions before customers book.

Admin panel

The owner manages bookings, customers, blog posts and settings from a private admin panel.

03 / Built with

The tools,
