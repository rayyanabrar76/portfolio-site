Who is Rayyan Abrar?

Rayyan Abrar is a full stack web developer based in Lahore, Pakistan. Rayyan builds websites, online stores, booking platforms and AI apps for businesses, and works remotely with clients around the world.

What websites has Rayyan Abrar built?

Client work includes the website for Advanced Power Solutions (APS), a generator, solar and UPS company in Lahore; a booking website in four languages for Gull Swiss Limousine & Tours in Switzerland; and a catering website for Chef Aboud Küche in Berlin. Rayyan also built Sellwavy, an online store builder, and Naro, an AI video studio.

Does Rayyan Abrar work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. Rayyan works remotely with businesses worldwide, including clients in Switzerland and Germany as well as Pakistan, and is available for freelance projects through WhatsApp, email and Upwork.

What does Rayyan Abrar build websites with?

Mostly Next.js and React, with Tailwind CSS for design, PostgreSQL or Supabase for data, and Stripe for online payments. Sites are built to load fast, work well on phones and be easy for Google to read.

Can I update my website myself?

Yes. Rayyan can build an admin panel into your website, like the ones made for APS and Gull Swiss Limousine & Tours, so you can manage products, bookings, customers or blog posts without a developer.
